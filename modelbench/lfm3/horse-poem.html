Into the Heart of the Barn

The Whispering Hay: Deep within a quiet valley, an old barn stands as much like a sleepy dragon’s lair. Its weathered wooden walls have seen countless seasons.

The Morning Ritual: At sunrise, golden light streams through cracked windows, and Starlight the horse stretches her delicate ears, feeling the cool straw beneath her hooves.

A Tale of Freedom: When storm clouds threatened to flood the lower lofts, Starlight trotted to the water barrel, nudging the heavy lid until the rain passed safely.

The Heart of the Barn: As twilight paints the sky in amber, fireflies dance on the rafters. Starlight rests beside a pile of soft hay, eyes half‑closed in contentment.
